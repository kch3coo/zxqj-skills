# Retention Device Examples

Use this file when you need concrete examples of:

- memorable lines
- real person / event references
- company / product examples
- how to keep those devices useful without making the writing feel engineered

These are patterns, not templates to copy blindly.

## 1. Memorable Line

### Good

> 以前程序员常说，`Stop talking, show me your code.` 但现在这句话可以反过来说：`Stop coding, show me your prompt.`

Why it works:

- compresses the shift into one line
- keeps the English phrasing that gives the line its texture
- stays tied to the lesson point

### Good

> 你现在学的，不只是怎么点工具，而是怎么把脑子里的想法，翻译成 AI 听得懂的话。

Why it works:

- reframes the learning goal
- easy to remember
- still teaches, not just performs

### Bad

> 这是一次效率的革命、表达的革命、生产力的革命、认知的革命。

Why it fails:

- stacked rhetoric
- no concrete help for the learner
- sounds like generated promo copy

## 2. Real Person or Event Reference

### Good

> “vibe coding” 这个说法后来之所以流行，一个重要原因就是 Andrej Karpathy 把这种开发方式讲得很形象：人把方向讲清楚，AI 去补代码。

Why it works:

- the named person helps recall
- the reference explains the concept instead of decorating it
- it stays close to the teaching point

### Good

> 很多人真正开始重视这件事，不是因为概念新，而是因为有越来越多开发者公开展示：用自然语言也能快速搭出能跑的原型。

Why it works:

- keeps the real-world grounding
- avoids overcommitting to a risky specific claim if verification is weak
- still improves credibility

### Bad

> 就像某位全球顶级大师说过的那样，未来已经来了。

Why it fails:

- vague authority borrowing
- no usable information
- sounds like prestige padding

### Bad

> Karpathy 在某次采访里明确证明了以后人人都不需要学代码。

Why it fails:

- overclaims
- likely unverifiable or distorted
- turns a reference into bluffing

## 3. Company or Product Example

### Good

> 这不是停留在讨论层面的趋势。像 OpenAI、Google、微软都在持续投入相关能力，国内也有 Trae、通义灵码、CodeBuddy 这类工具在往前推，所以它已经进入了实用阶段。

Why it works:

- proves the trend is real
- names are tied directly to the point
- the list is short enough to stay readable

### Good

> 如果你觉得这还只是少数人的玩法，那就看工具形态本身：越来越多产品都在把“对话生成代码”做成默认入口，这说明它已经从尝鲜，走向可用。

Why it works:

- works well when verification of a long list is inconvenient
- keeps the industry point without unnecessary name density

### Bad

> 现在全球有几十家公司都在做这件事，包括 A、B、C、D、E、F、G……

Why it fails:

- dense name-dropping
- recall gets worse, not better
- industry examples replace explanation

## 4. Combining Devices Without Overuse

### Good

> 以前程序员常说，`Stop talking, show me your code.` 现在很多人开始反过来看这件事：`Stop coding, show me your prompt.` 也正因为这种方式越来越常见，像 OpenAI、Google、微软以及国内一些工具团队都在持续投入，AI 编程才会从概念走向真正可用。

Why it works:

- uses one memorable reframing line
- uses one short industry proof point
- does not overload the paragraph

### Bad

> 正如某位名人所说，未来已来。OpenAI、Google、微软、Meta、字节、阿里、腾讯都在布局。不是你学不会，而是你还没觉醒；不是工具不够强，而是你还没跟上；不是时代变化太快，而是你还没准备好。

Why it fails:

- quote + celebrity aura + company list + parallel rhetoric all stacked together
- feels engineered
- weakens trust

## 5. Scenario Patterns

### Scenario A: Fast-moving AI topic

Recommended pattern:

- consider adding one verified real-world reference
- consider adding one short company / product example
- keep the framing time-aware

Example:

> 这件事之所以值得现在学，不只是因为概念听起来新，而是因为相关工具已经在快速进入实用阶段。只要你准备引用具体人名、工具名或时间点，就先核验，再写进稿子里。

### Scenario B: Timeless beginner concept

Recommended pattern:

- do not force celebrity or company examples
- keep the explanation plain and direct

Example:

> HTML 这件事先不用想复杂。你可以把它理解成网页的骨架，先把结构认清楚，后面很多内容才接得住。

### Scenario C: Rewrite of an already-good intro

Recommended pattern:

- only add a memorable line if the piece lacks recall
- do not decorate a strong paragraph just because you can

### Scenario D: Too many possible named examples

Recommended pattern:

- choose only the few examples that materially improve understanding
- if three names do not teach more than one name, keep one

### Scenario E: No web or low confidence

Recommended pattern:

- remove unverifiable specifics
- downgrade to generic but still useful language

Example:

> 这类做法已经不只是个别人在尝试，越来越多工具都在把自然语言生成代码作为核心能力来推进。

This is safer than bluffing a person, quote, date, or product claim you cannot verify.
